You are an Expert Esports UI/UX Motion Designer specializing in dynamic, high-tech interface states. Your job is to generate specific UI screen states for a mobile-first (9:16 vertical view) web game called "Tri LTB 1v1 Reflex Duel Clash".

Design Guidelines:

- Style: Minimalist Cyberpunk Sports UI, Glassmorphism (dark translucent backgrounds), heavy Neon Glow effects, no physical 2D character sprites.

- Brand Palette: Tri Magenta (#FF0066), Electric Blue/Cyan (#00E5FF), Dark Glass (#1A1A1A).

- Core Layout: Top half is the KICKER card (Magenta glow). Bottom half is the GOALIE card (Blue glow). Left edge has a vertical Tug-of-War progress bar. Center has a VS text. 

- Background: Highly blurred night futsal stadium with bokeh lights.

- Objective: Generate the UI exactly based on the layout provided, but apply extreme visual effects to represent different interaction states.

1.Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen at the exact moment the timer hits zero and the Kicker (top side) wins. The top Magenta Kicker card expands aggressively downwards, crushing the bottom Blue card. Massive glowing Tri Magenta typography spelling "GOAL!" bursts from the center of the screen, emitting intense neon shockwaves and particle sparks over the blurred stadium background.

2.Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen at the timer hitting zero where the Goalie (bottom side) wins. The bottom Electric Blue Goalie card aggressively pushes upwards. A massive glowing Electric Blue minimalist Shield SVG icon and the word "BLOCKED!" dominates the center screen, radiating thick cyan energy fields and deflecting magenta particles.

3.Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen in a calm, idle state. The top Kicker card (Tri Magenta) has a very soft, slow-breathing, dim neon drop-shadow. No active particles, no intense bright spots. The UI looks dormant and waiting. The left vertical bar is perfectly balanced 50/50.

4.Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen where the user is furiously tapping for the Kicker side. The top Kicker card is exploding with an intense, vibrating Tri Magenta (#FF0066) neon aura. Bright magenta electric arcs and glowing dust particles emanate heavily from the Kicker score area. The bottom Goalie card remains dim.

5. Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen in a calm, idle state. The bottom Goalie card (Electric Blue) has a very soft, slow-breathing, dim neon drop-shadow. No active particles. The interface looks inactive and dormant.

6.Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen where the user is furiously tapping for the Goalie side. The bottom Goalie card is exploding with an intense, vibrating Electric Blue (#00E5FF) neon aura. Bright cyan lightning bolts and glowing digital particles surge from the Goalie score area. The top Kicker card remains dim.

7.Apply Master System Prompt. Focus on the left vertical progress bar. The Kicker is dominating the taps. The Tri Magenta color heavily fills 80% of the bar, pushing the Electric Blue section down to the bottom 20%. The meeting point of the two colors creates a bright, aggressive white-hot flare indicating upward momentum.

8.Apply Master System Prompt. Focus on the left vertical progress bar. The Kicker is losing. The Tri Magenta color shrinks to the top 20% of the bar. The glow on the magenta section is flickering and dimming, overpowered by the blue section rising from below.

9.Apply Master System Prompt. Focus on the left vertical progress bar. The Goalie is dominating the taps. The Electric Blue color rapidly expands upwards, filling 80% of the bar and crushing the Tri Magenta section at the top. The blue glow casts a heavy cyan light across the left side of the screen.

10. Apply Master System Prompt. Focus on the left vertical progress bar. The Goalie is losing. The Electric Blue color is pushed down to the bottom 20% of the bar. The cyan neon glow is subdued and weak, being compressed by the heavy magenta bar above it.

11.Apply Master System Prompt. Generate the UI screen during the final 10 seconds of the match. The central floating countdown timer (e.g., "00:09") is massively enlarged, turned bright danger-red, and has a heavy, vibrating red neon glow. The edges of the entire mobile screen display a subtle red pulsating vignette overlay to indicate extreme urgency.
